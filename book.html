Your home by the lake

Pangea Resort is a quiet lakeside resort in Kathu, one minute's walk from the Phuket King ring. Eleven air-conditioned bungalows sit in two areas, Mango Grove and Lake View, each with a comfortable bed, an en-suite bathroom and its own outdoor space. No taxis and no commute: wake up, walk to class, and come back to the lake. Pangea Cafe cooks healthy meals on site, the ice baths and herbal sauna are next door, and group Muay Thai is included with every stay.

  • One minute walk to the gym
  • Air conditioning and en-suite bathroom
  • Your own outdoor space
  • Group Muay Thai included
  • Healthy food at Pangea Cafe
  • Ice baths and herbal sauna
