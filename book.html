Pangea brings it all back together

Most trips split your life into pieces: a hotel here, a gym across town, whatever food you can find. Pangea brings it all back together. Where you sleep, how you train, how you recover, what you eat and how you feel, all in one place, around one lake. You are in the centre of Phuket, yet it feels like somewhere completely different, far from the hustle and bustle.

Stay

Private, air-conditioned bungalows by the lake, each with its own bathroom and plenty of privacy. Comfortable mattresses with queen or king size bedding.

Fitness

Phuket King Muay Thai is on the same lake, a 2 minute walk away. Group classes are included with your stay, so you can train whenever you want.

Recovery

Ice baths and a herbal sauna next door, quiet nights and real sleep, so you are ready to go again tomorrow.

Nutrition

Pangea Cafe makes clean, healthy food fresh on site, including juices, drinks and salads. Tell us your aim (lose weight, gain weight or build muscle) and we build your meal plan around it.

Wellness

Wellness starts in the mind. Pangea is a place to reset, and it feels like a little village: a small community of people who came here to train, eat well and feel better.

Your home by the lake

Pangea Resort is built around the same lake as the gym, in two zones: Lake View and Mango Grove. Every bungalow is private, air-conditioned and en-suite, right next to the lake, with a comfortable queen or king size bed. You are surrounded by water and nature, with banana trees, coconut palms and mango trees all around, yet you are a stone's throw from the main Muay Thai gym. Need to work? Good internet makes remote working easy. No taxis and no commute: wake up, walk to class, and come back to the lake.
