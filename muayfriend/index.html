What it does

Everything a gym runs on, in one place. Use only what you need.

The front desk

Fast at the desk, clear for new staff.

  • Check-in by name, phone or email, with a self check-in desk mode
  • Packages and memberships with visit limits, expiry and renewal reminders
  • Walk-ins and agency guests sold and checked in at once
  • Timetable with classes you can pause for a day or a week
  • Privates: requests, approval, packs, moves and coach pay
  • Payments by cash, card link or PromptPay QR

Coaching

Real coaching between classes, not just attendance.

  • Workout programs with sets, reps, supersets and left and right sides
  • Templates you can give to any client in one tap
  • Habits, food diary and weight, including fight weight cuts
  • Progress charts from the weights members log themselves
  • Form checks: members film a lift or a combo, coaches reply
  • Action groups like Fight camp, with steps that unlock week by week

Selling and marketing

Bring people in without paying for five other tools.

  • Online shop and package sales on your website
  • Video courses with free preview lessons
  • Newsletters to everyone or by tag, with unsubscribe built in
  • Social media posts written by AI from your photos, approved by you, posted on schedule
  • Web pages made by describing them, then edited by hand
  • Google review requests on WhatsApp, in one tap

Running the business

Know where the money goes.

  • Receipts photographed or uploaded, read by AI
  • Accounts by business and department, with monthly bills
  • Staff hours and commission worked out for you
  • Investor view with totals only, never member details
  • Notices and tasks that pass between staff
  • Nightly backups of everything

Rooms and stays

For camps with accommodation.

  • Online booking page with live room availability and extras
  • Room calendars synced with Booking.com and Agoda
  • Room bills with training, food and extras in one place
  • Housekeeping on the cleaners' phones, also in Thai and Burmese
  • Welcome emails sent before guests arrive
  • Kitchen screen for cafe orders

Built in a real camp

Muay Friend was built inside Phuket King Muay Thai, a lakeside training camp in Kathu, Phuket, where it runs the whole business every day.

Every feature started as a real problem at the desk, in the ring or in the office. If it didn't save the team time, it didn't stay.

Questions

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It works on any phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to install from an app store: open the link and add it to the home screen.

Is my members' data private?

Each gym gets its own private database, separate from every other gym. Staff only see what their role needs, and everything is backed up every night.

Which payments can it take?

Cash at the desk, card payment links, and PromptPay QR in Thailand. Members can also pay in their own app.

Can I use only some of it?

Yes. We switch off what you don't need, so a gym without rooms won't see stays or housekeeping.

Can you move my existing members across?

Yes. Send us your current list and we'll bring in your members, packages and what they have left.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your gym and what you use. Tell us about your gym and we'll send you a clear monthly price.
