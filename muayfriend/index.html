Made by people who run gyms

Muay Friend was made by a small team who have run Muay Thai gyms, camps and resorts for years, and worked with many other gyms and businesses along the way. We used everything: a CRM, a website with someone to look after it, booking plugins, gym apps, a coaching app, accounting software, spreadsheets and LINE groups. Each did one job, none of them talked to each other, and none were made for a Muay Thai gym.

So we built the one we wanted. Made for Muay Thai and MMA gyms, not one size fits all. It thinks about the little things you only know if you run a gym: privates and coach commission, walk-ins paying cash, fight teams, rooms and the cafe, Thai and foreign customers.

It has been shaped by owners, managers and coaches from over 30 gyms around the world, and tested every day with real members, coaches and front desks. The aim is simple: everything together, affordable, and a great experience for the gym, the staff and the customers.

  • Save money. One app instead of a stack of monthly subscriptions.
  • Save time. Check-ins in seconds, payments recorded as you sell, accounts sorted for you.
  • Easy for coaches. Simple screens, no big learning curve. Each coach only sees what they need.
  • Control from your phone. See today, approve privates and check the money from anywhere.
  • Your data is yours. Kept private and separate from every other gym. Download it any time. We never contact your members and never share or sell anything.

Questions

Will my coaches find it hard to learn?

No. The screens are simple and each role only sees what it needs, so there's no big learning curve. Key staff screens also work in Thai and Burmese.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It works on any phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to install from an app store: open the link and add it to the home screen.

Is my members' data private?

Yes. Your gym's data is kept completely separate from every other gym: paid plans get their own private database, and free gyms get their own private section. No other gym can see your members, prices or numbers. We never contact your members, and we never share or sell your data. You can download everything any time, and if you leave we delete it. Inside your gym, staff only see what their role needs, and everything is backed up every night.

Who is behind Muay Friend?

A small team who have run Muay Thai gyms, camps and resorts for years, with a Thai team for Thai gyms. We made Muay Friend because nothing else was built for how a Muay Thai gym really works. Muay Friend is run as its own business: your data never goes to any other gym, and we never take sides between gyms.

Which payments can it take?

Cash at the desk, PromptPay QR straight to your bank with no fees, and card payments through Stripe in the app and by link. More Thai payment options, including TrueMoney and Rabbit LINE Pay, are on the way.

Can I use only some of it?

Yes. We switch off what you don't need, so a gym without rooms won't see stays or housekeeping.

Can you move my existing members across?

Yes. Send us your current list and we'll bring in your members, packages and what they have left.

Is the AI included?

The AI tools are an optional add-on: making web pages from a description, writing social media posts from your photos, and reading receipts. Everything else works without them, and you can add them any time.

How much does it cost?

Small gyms can start free. Paid plans start at $45 a month, with 50% off your first 2 months at launch. See Prices.

What is in the free version?

Check-ins, packages, selling and appointments, with your gym's name, for up to 15 members and 2 staff. It shows a small Muay Friend logo. You can move up to a paid plan any time and keep everything.
