Built with gyms, for gyms

Working closely with gyms, we noticed how many separate apps the average gym needs just to run. They creep up one at a time. Some clash, some don't talk to each other, and that's when things slip: a missed booking, a check-in that never got recorded, a renewal nobody chased.

So we made Muay Friend to keep it simple. Everything in one place, so you know where your staff are, you know where your customers are, and the whole team finds it easy to use.

It was built with the help of over 30 gyms around the world. We asked owners, managers and coaches real questions about how they work and what gets in their way, then built the answers.

  • Save money. One app instead of a stack of monthly subscriptions.
  • Save time. Check-ins in seconds, payments recorded as you sell, accounts sorted for you.
  • Easy for coaches. Simple screens, no big learning curve. Each coach only sees what they need.
  • Control from your phone. See today, approve privates and check the money from anywhere.
  • Your data stays yours. Every gym has its own private database. No other gym can ever see it.

Questions

Will my coaches find it hard to learn?

No. The screens are simple and each role only sees what it needs, so there's no big learning curve. Key staff screens also work in Thai and Burmese.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It works on any phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to install from an app store: open the link and add it to the home screen.

Is my members' data private?

Yes. Each gym gets its own private database, completely separate from every other gym. No other gym can see your members, prices or numbers, and we never share or sell your data. Inside your gym, staff only see what their role needs, and everything is backed up every night.

Which payments can it take?

Cash at the desk, card payment links, and PromptPay QR in Thailand. Members can also pay in their own app.

Can I use only some of it?

Yes. We switch off what you don't need, so a gym without rooms won't see stays or housekeeping.

Can you move my existing members across?

Yes. Send us your current list and we'll bring in your members, packages and what they have left.

Is the AI included?

The AI tools are an optional add-on: making web pages from a description, writing social media posts from your photos, and reading receipts. Everything else works without them, and you can add them any time.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your gym and what you use. Tell us about your gym and we'll send you a clear monthly price.
