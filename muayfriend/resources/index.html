Connect Facebook and Instagram

Messages from your Facebook page and Instagram come into Messages, and Social media posts to both. About 5 minutes.

Before you start

  • Your gym has a Facebook page and you are an admin of it (full control).
  • Your Instagram is a professional account (business or creator). In the Instagram app: your profile, the menu, Account type and tools, Switch to professional account.
  • Your Instagram is linked to the Facebook page. In the Instagram app: Edit profile, Page, and pick your page. Or in Meta Business Suite: Settings, Instagram accounts, Connect.
  • You are signed in to the app as the owner or a manager, on a computer if you can (the Meta window is easier on a big screen).

  1. Open Messages. At the top, tap Connect Facebook and Instagram. You can also start from Social media.

  2. Tap Sign in with Meta. A Meta window opens. If nothing happens, allow pop ups for the app and tap it again.

  3. In the Meta window: sign in with the Facebook account that is an admin of the page, tap Continue, pick your business if asked, then tick your page and your Instagram account. Leave every permission on and tap Save.

  4. If your account manages more than one page, the app asks which one. Pick your gym's page and tap Connect.

  5. Done. Messages shows Connected with your page and Instagram name. New Facebook and Instagram messages now come into Messages, and your team gets a phone notice.

For Instagram messages, one more switch

In the Instagram app: Settings, Messages and story replies, Message controls, Connected tools, and turn on Allow access to messages. Without it, Instagram messages stay in Instagram only.

Good to know

  • Meta lets businesses reply within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that, the app says it could not be delivered.
  • To use a different page, tap Change Facebook page. To stop, tap Disconnect Facebook.
  • Posting from Social media uses the same connection. Nothing is posted until a manager approves it.

Connect WhatsApp

WhatsApp chats come into Messages next to Facebook, Instagram and website chat. Your number can stay on the WhatsApp Business app on your phone. About 10 minutes.

Before you start

  • Your number is on the WhatsApp Business app (not the normal WhatsApp app), version 2.24.17 or newer. Update it in the App Store or Google Play first.
  • Have the phone with that WhatsApp in your hand: you scan a code with it.
  • A Facebook account you use for the business. If you connected Facebook above, use the same one.
  • Signed in to the app as the owner or a manager, ideally on a computer.

  1. Open Messages and tap Connect WhatsApp.

  2. Choose Keep it on the WhatsApp Business app on my phone too, so you can still use WhatsApp on the phone. Then tap Sign in with Meta.

  3. In the Meta window: sign in, tap Get started, pick your business (or create one with your gym's name), and choose Connect your existing WhatsApp Business app.

  4. Type your WhatsApp number. Meta shows a QR code. On the phone, WhatsApp Business shows a message about connecting: follow it and scan the code. Choose to share your chat history so recent chats come into the app too.

  5. Done. Messages shows WhatsApp Connected with your number. Tap Check any time to see that it is working.

Good to know

  • Replies you send from the phone also show in the app, and replies from the app show on the phone.
  • WhatsApp lets businesses reply within 24 hours of the customer's last message.
  • Keep using the WhatsApp Business app at least once every 14 days, or WhatsApp may disconnect it from the app.
  • A brand new number not on any phone? Choose A number only for the app instead.

Turn on AI replies

After connecting, the AI can answer WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram straight away with your prices and class times. About 3 minutes.

  1. In Messages, tap AI replies. Set AI replies to On and tick where it answers: WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram.

  2. Choose When: at all times, or only when your website chat shows Away (outside desk hours).

  3. Write Notes for the AI: your address and how to get there, opening hours, what to bring, and anything else people ask. Prices and the timetable come from the app by themselves. Tap Save.

Good to know

  • When a staff member replies in a chat, the AI stays quiet there.
  • For bookings, payments, complaints or anything it does not know, it tells the customer the team will reply and sends your desk a phone notice.

Connect YouTube and TikTok

Post your reels to YouTube Shorts and TikTok from Social media, with Facebook and Instagram. About 2 minutes each.

  1. Open Social media. Facebook and Instagram connect here too, the same way as in Messages.

  2. Tap Connect YouTube. Google opens: sign in with the account that owns your YouTube channel, pick the channel, and allow the app to upload videos. You come back to the app and it shows your channel name.

  3. Tap Connect TikTok. Sign in with your business TikTok account and allow everything asked. Your videos go to your TikTok drafts: open TikTok to add a sound and post.

Good to know

  • When you make a post, tick where it goes. A reel can go to all four at once.
  • Videos under about 60 MB send best.
