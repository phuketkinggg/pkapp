The philosophy

Most wellness ends when you fly home. Ours starts there.

A week away feels good. Then real life takes over and nothing has changed. A retreat, an escape, a one-time visit: none of them stay with you.

Pangea is built the other way round. We help you build a lifestyle you can keep, and we are always there while you live it. One team that knows your body, your habits and your goals, and looks after all of it in a joined-up way.

Our story

It started with a gym.

Phuket King Muay Thai opened in Kathu, Phuket with one aim: teach Muay Thai properly to anyone willing to work, from first timers to fighters.

Around the gym grew lakeside bungalows, a cafe that cooks with no processed seed oils, a recovery zone and a wellness store. Piece by piece it became a place where every part of feeling well lives side by side.

That needed a name bigger than a gym, and an idea bigger than a holiday. We called it Pangea.

Tom Watkins, founder
