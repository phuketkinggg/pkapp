PEOPLE | PLACES | LONGER LIVES

Once there was one land. You were once one, too.

Two hundred and fifty million years ago, every continent on Earth was joined as a single landmass. Geologists call it Pangea. Over time it broke apart and drifted, until the pieces no longer looked like they belonged together.

Modern health care has done the same to people. We are split into symptoms and illnesses, sent from one specialist to the next, and the body is treated as muscles, ligaments and tendons instead of one living thing. Nobody looks at the whole of you.

Pangea puts it back together. Your mind, your body, what you eat, your blood and your genes, looked after as one, by one team that knows you.

Built for one world. Living in another.

For almost all of human history we walked, carried, hunted, slept with the sun and ate what the land gave us. Our bodies are still built for that life. Chairs, screens, processed food, artificial light and constant stress arrived in the last few moments of our story.

Scientists call it the evolutionary mismatch, and it sits behind most of the chronic illness of our time. Pangea closes the gap: we bring back what the body expects, then measure that it works.
