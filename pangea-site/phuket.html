Why Muay Thai

The sport is the vessel, not the destination.

You are not coming here to become a fighter. You do not need to be aggressive, and you do not need to know anything about Muay Thai. It is the craft that carries you to your own wellness and leaves you feeling stronger in yourself.

This is not a Muay Thai camp. It is still Pangea: every part of your wellness, looked after as one. We chose Muay Thai for Phuket because of what it does for a person, women and men alike.

Train · The craft

Phuket King Muay Thai

One of the best known Muay Thai gyms in Thailand, famous for its technical approach to coaching. That approach is why it works for people who have never trained before.

Fighters train here too, but you do not have to be one. Now on a new lakeside site of more than 2,000 m², with a different focus every day. Private sessions and kids classes run alongside the group classes. Work hard, fight easy.

  • Full size ring and 30 heavy bags
  • New free weights zone
  • Full spa area: herbal sauna, dry sauna and ice bath
  • On-site cafe and lakeside bungalows
  • Supplement and equipment shop

Recover

Spa and recovery.

A full spa area beside the gym. Hard training should be matched with proper recovery: heat, cold and somewhere quiet to slow down.

  • Herbal sauna: a traditional herbal steam sauna to sweat it out after training
  • Dry sauna: dry heat for when you want it hotter and simpler
  • Ice bath: cold water to finish the session
  • Chill deck: for breathwork, stretching or simply slowing down
