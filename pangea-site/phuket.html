The sport is the vessel, not the destination.

You are not coming here to become a fighter. You do not need to be aggressive, and you do not need to know anything about Muay Thai. It is the craft that carries you to your own wellness, and it leaves you stronger in yourself.

This is not a Muay Thai camp. It is Pangea: every part of you, looked after as one. We chose Muay Thai for Phuket because of what it does for a person, women and men alike.

ConfidenceIt grows with every session you finish.
ResilienceMental and physical, and it goes home with you.
Balance and rotationThe whole body learns to turn, shift and hold.
MobilityMoving freely through your full range.
Joint strengthStrength where the body needs it most.
EmpowermentSelf-improvement that reaches well beyond the gym.

Heat, cold and somewhere quiet.

A small lakeside spa beside the gym, because hard work only pays off with proper recovery.
