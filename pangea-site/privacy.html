Privacy Policy

How Pangea Global Wellness and the Pangea app look after your personal and health data. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Who we are

Pangea Global Wellness ("Pangea", "we", "us") is run by Phuket King Sports and Leisure in Kathu, Phuket, Thailand, together with Phuket King Muay Thai, Pangea Resort and Pangea Cafe. This policy covers this website (pangeaglobalwellness.com) and the Pangea app (app.pangeaglobalwellness.com, and the Pangea app in the App Store and Google Play). We follow Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA). If you are in the EU or UK, the rights in the GDPR also apply to you.

Questions or requests: hello@phuketking.com, or WhatsApp +66 96 907 6150.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email and password (stored securely, we never see it), and your phone number if you give it.
  • Your membership: your level, packages, bookings, payments and what is still to pay.
  • Health and wellness information you choose to share: goals, habits, measurements, food and wellness diary, photos, blood test results, doctor's letters, scans and notes from your coaches. This is sensitive data. We only keep it after you agree in the app, only your coaches and our managers can see it, and you can take back your agreement or ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Messages you send your coach or our team in the app.
  • Payments: card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Technical information needed to keep you signed in and the service secure, and, if you turn them on, a code for phone notifications. We do not use advertising trackers in the app.

Why we use it

  • To provide your membership, coaching and bookings (our contract with you).
  • So your coaches can look after you, with your consent for health information.
  • To reply to your messages and send you notifications you turned on.
  • To keep accounts and tax records (a legal obligation).
  • To send news and offers, only if you agreed. You can stop them at any time.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with other businesses for their marketing.

Who we share it with

Only with the services we use to run Pangea, and only what they need:

  • Supabase: secure hosting for the app and database (servers in Singapore).
  • Stripe: card payments.
  • Resend: sending our emails.
  • Apple and Google: delivering phone notifications you turned on.
  • Phuket King Muay Thai, Pangea Resort and Pangea Cafe: our own businesses at Pangea Phuket, when you train, stay or eat with us. It is the same account.
  • Thai government authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these services store data outside Thailand. When that happens we use providers with proper safeguards for international transfers.

How long we keep it

  • Payments and receipts: as long as Thai accounting and tax law requires, usually up to 5 years.
  • Your account, health and coaching information: while you are a member, then deleted when you delete your account or ask us to.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, take back your consent, or object to how we use it. You can delete your account yourself in the app: Me, Delete my account. This deletes your login, contact details and everything in your file and coaching. We keep a record of past payments without your name, because the law requires it. You can also email us and we will do it for you. If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access limited to you, your coaches and our managers. Health documents are kept in private storage that only you and your coaches can open.

Children

The Pangea app is for adults. Children's training at Phuket King is booked by a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
