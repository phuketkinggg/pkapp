About Phuket King

More than
a Muay Thai gym.

A place people come to reset, rebuild and reconnect with themselves through movement, discipline, recovery and community.

Why we built it

Something deeper than a training camp

In the heart of Phuket, we brought authentic, technique-first Muay Thai together with modern recovery, healthy food and a lakeside place to stay, all designed to help you grow physically and mentally.

Complete beginner, active fighter, fitness traveller, or simply looking for a healthier and more focused way to live. Wherever you are starting from, there is a place for you here.

Everything in one place

Train. Recover. Stay. Fuel.

Recovery, accommodation and proper food, all within a minute's walk of the ring.

01

Train

Three classes a day, each with a different technique focus: pad work, bag work, sparring and clinch. A full-size ring, 15 heavy bags and a free weights area.

02

Recover

Ice baths, a traditional herbal sauna, and a chill deck for stretching, breathwork and yoga. Recover today so you can train again tomorrow.

03

Stay

Eleven air-conditioned lakeside bungalows at Pangea Resort, each with an en-suite bathroom and its own outdoor space. One minute from the gym.

04

Fuel

Pangea Cafe cooks healthy meals without seed oils, with good coffee, fresh smoothies and protein shakes. Our store stocks protein, hydration and vitamins.

A typical day

Train hard, live well

  • Morning Muay Thai
  • Breakfast and a smoothie at Pangea Cafe
  • Ice bath and sauna
  • Strength or mobility work
  • An afternoon by the lake
  • Dinner with people from all over the world
Who comes here

Some guests come to fight. Others come to lose weight, rebuild confidence, recover from burnout or build better habits. Many arrive for a short stay and leave having changed far more than their fitness.

What makes us different

Balance. Hard training matched with proper recovery. Discipline alongside enjoyment. A community built on respect, humility and consistency.

Everyone is welcome

Whatever your background or level, your first class or your hundredth, our coaches and community help you grow at your own pace.

The people

You are becoming family

What makes Phuket King special is the people. Guests come from all over the world, for all kinds of reasons. What they share is the atmosphere, and a feeling of belonging that grows inside and outside the gym. For many, it becomes a second home.
