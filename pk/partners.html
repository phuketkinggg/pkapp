Partner with Phuket King

Love training here, or have an audience that would? Send people our way and earn a share of every stay, training package and DTV visa course booked through your own link.

How it worksEvery partner gets their own link and QR code. When someone books through it, it is tracked in our app, and you can see every booking and what you have earned, live. You are paid monthly, once the guest has come.

Three levels

Choose the level that fits your reach. We check every application by hand, because the right audience matters more than the biggest number.

How to join

  1. Apply below. Tell us where your audience is and roughly how big it is.
  2. We look at your channels. We check your audience, your engagement and that you fit Phuket King: friendly, family, respect.
  3. You get your link. Approved partners get their link, their QR code and their level, usually within a few days.
  4. Share and earn. Every booking through your link is tracked, and you are paid monthly.

Good to know

What can people book through my link?

Rooms at Pangea Resort, training packages and courses, and our DTV visa courses. You earn your share of the room or package price, not the extras.

How long does my link count?

When someone opens your link, bookings they make on our website over the following weeks are tagged to you, even if they come back later.

When am I paid?

Monthly, once the guest has come. Cancelled or refunded bookings do not count.

Do Creators and Ambassadors have to post?

Yes. Free training and stays are a content partnership. We agree what you will post before you come, and the next deal depends on it.

I trained here before. Can I join?

Yes, past guests can always join as an Affiliate, whatever their follower count. You are often our best ambassadors.
