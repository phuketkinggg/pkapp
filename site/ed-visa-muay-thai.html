The visa in short

What the ED visa gives you

The Non-Immigrant ED visa for Muay Thai lets you stay in Thailand and train for 3 months (90 days). Learning Muay Thai at a registered gym counts as study.

  • What you need: a Muay Thai course of 3 months or more with us.
  • Your invitation: we send your details to the Sports Authority of Thailand (SAT). They check you are eligible and send you an invitation letter to come and train. This takes about 3 to 4 weeks.
  • Letter fee: 3,000 THB for the Sports Authority letter, paid to us with your course.
  • Where you apply: from outside Thailand, online at thaievisa.go.th. You need to be in another country when you apply.
  • Visa fee: about 1,800 THB, paid to the Thai e-visa website.
  • How long the embassy takes: between 3 and 10 days, depending on the embassy.
  • Approval: with the Sports Authority letter the visa is usually approved, because your eligibility is already confirmed.
  • Work: not allowed on an ED visa, and that includes remote work. If you need to work online, the DTV is the better fit.

You do not have to be a fighter. We coach complete beginners, fitness students and fighters.

  2. Send us your detailsMessage us on WhatsApp with your passport details and when you want to start. We send everything to the Sports Authority of Thailand.
  3. Get your invitationThe Sports Authority replies in about 3 to 4 weeks and sends you an invitation letter to come and train with us.
  4. Apply from outside ThailandFrom another country, apply online at thaievisa.go.th for the 3 month ED visa for Muay Thai and pay the visa fee of about 1,800 THB. The embassy takes 3 to 10 days.
  5. Fly in and trainOnce your visa is approved, fly into Thailand and start training. Your 90 days start when you arrive.
Timing

Start early

The whole process takes about 4 to 6 weeks: 3 to 4 weeks for the Sports Authority letter, then 3 to 10 days for the embassy. Some embassies are really quick and some are slow, so start at least 6 weeks before you want to begin training.

Want to stay longer? An ED visa cannot be extended inside Thailand. You apply for a new ED visa: we ask the Sports Authority for a new letter, and you apply again from outside Thailand. Start about a month before your current visa runs out.

Documents

What you need to apply

ED visa or DTV?

ED visa suits you if you want to train for 3 months and not work while you are here. The fees are low. For longer, you apply for a new ED visa from outside Thailand.

DTV suits you if you want longer, or you work online. It is a 5 year visa with stays of up to 180 days per entry, and it allows remote work. You need to be 20 or older and show about 500,000 THB in savings, and the visa fee is higher.

Not sure? Read our DTV page or message us and we will tell you which one fits.

Questions about the ED visa

Do I need Muay Thai experience?

No. We coach beginners, people who want fitness, and fighters.

How long does the whole process take?

About 4 to 6 weeks. The Sports Authority of Thailand usually takes 3 to 4 weeks to send your invitation, then the embassy takes 3 to 10 days. Start early.

Do I have to leave Thailand to apply?

Yes. The ED visa is applied for from outside Thailand, online at thaievisa.go.th.

Will my visa be approved?

It is usually approved once you have the Sports Authority letter, because they have already confirmed you are eligible. The embassy makes the final decision.

How long can I stay?

90 days from the day you arrive.

Can I extend it?

Not inside Thailand. You apply for a new ED visa: we ask the Sports Authority for a new letter, and you apply again from outside Thailand. Start about a month before your current visa runs out.

What does it cost?

Your 3 month course, the 3,000 THB Sports Authority letter fee, and about 1,800 THB visa fee on the Thai e-visa website.

Can I work on an ED visa?

No. Work of any kind is not allowed, including remote work for clients abroad. Look at the DTV if you need to work.

Can I stay at the gym?

Yes. Pangea Resort lakeside bungalows are on site. See book your stay.

Are the visa rules fixed?

No. Thai immigration rules and fees can change. This page was checked in November 2026. Message us before you book flights and we will give you the current picture.
