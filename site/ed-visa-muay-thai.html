The visa in short

What the ED visa gives you

The Non-Immigrant ED visa is Thailand's education visa. Learning Muay Thai at a registered gym counts as study, so you can stay and train for months instead of weeks.

  • First stay: 90 days from the day you arrive.
  • Extending: Possible, but via the same pricess.
  • Longest stay: up to 1 year in total from your first entry. After that you leave Thailand and apply again.
  • Visa fee: about 2,000 THB (around 80 USD or EUR) when you apply. It changes slightly by embassy.
  • Work: not allowed on an ED visa, and that includes remote work. If you need to work online, the DTV is the better fit.

You do not have to be a fighter. We coach complete beginners, fitness students and fighters.

Documents

What you need to apply

  • Passport valid for at least 6 more months
  • A recent passport photo
  • Proof of money for your stay, usually a bank statement (around 20,000 THB is often asked)
  • Your enrolment letter from the gym
  • The letter from the education or sports authority that goes with it

Apply online at thaievisa.go.th if your country is on it, or at a Thai embassy or consulate. Processing usually takes 1 to 3 weeks, so start at least a month before you fly. We tell you exactly which documents to send and help with the gym side.

ED visa or DTV?

ED visa suits you if you want to train for 3 months to a year and not work while you are here. The visa fee is low and you extend every 90 days.

DTV suits you if you want longer, or you work online. It is a 5 year visa with stays of up to 180 days per entry, and it allows remote work. You need to be 20 or older and show about 500,000 THB in savings, and the visa fee is higher.

Questions about the ED visa

Do I need Muay Thai experience?

No. We coach beginners, people who want fitness, and fighters.

How long can I stay?

90 days on arrival. You can extend inside Thailand while you keep training, up to 1 year in total from your first entry.

Can I extend in Phuket?

Yes, at Phuket Immigration. Bring your passport, a photo, your school letter, your attendance record, proof of where you stay and 1,900 THB in cash.

Can I work on an ED visa?

No. Work of any kind is not allowed, including remote work for clients abroad. Look at the DTV if you need to work.

Do I need to report my address?

If you stay more than 90 days in a row, you report your address to immigration every 90 days. It can usually be done online.

Can I stay at the gym?

Yes. Pangea Resort lakeside bungalows are on site. See book your stay.

Are the visa rules fixed?

No. Thai immigration rules and fees can change. This page was checked in October 2026. Message us before you book flights and we will give you the current picture.
