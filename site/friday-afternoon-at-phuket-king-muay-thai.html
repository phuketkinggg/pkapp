One Afternoon at Phuket King Muay Thai, Hour by Hour

What does a training afternoon at a Muay Thai gym in Phuket actually look like? We set a camera up on a Friday and let it run from 3:30 to 6:00. Here is the whole afternoon in 80 seconds, and what happens at each stage.

3:30 to 4:30: Fight Fitness

The afternoon starts with the fighters. Fight Fitness is an hour of conditioning: runs, circuits, footwork drills and the kind of work that keeps you strong in round five. It is part of our fight programme, and it is hard work.

4:30 to 5:00: fighters on the pads

While the mats are reset, the fighters move to the ring for pad work with the krus. Short, sharp rounds, with the coach calling the combinations and fixing the details.

4:35 to 4:40: tyres and skipping

Between rounds you will see tyres and skipping ropes come out. It is old school Thai conditioning and it still works.

4:50: hand wraps and shadow boxing

The group class arrives. People wrap their hands, loosen up and shadow box to get moving. Never wrapped your hands before? Just ask, one of the coaches will show you.

5:00 to 5:15: group technique

The whole group comes together for technique. This is where we are different: technique first, always. The coach shows one idea, breaks it down, and everyone drills it slowly before adding speed and power. Beginners and experienced people train side by side, each at their own level.

5:00 to 5:30: fighters on the bags

At the same time the fighters work the heavy bags along the lake side of the gym, building volume and power.

5:15 to 5:50: pad work and bag work

The group class splits up. Some people do rounds on the pads with the krus while others work the bags, then they swap. This is where the technique from earlier gets put to work.

5:30 to 6:00: sparring

The fighters finish with controlled sparring in the ring. Sparring is never forced on anyone in the group class. You spar when you are ready, and only if you want to.

5:50 to 6:00: warm down and clean up

Everyone finishes together with a warm down and stretch on the mats, and then the gym gets cleaned for the evening class. After that, plenty of people walk over to the ice bath and sauna, or grab food at the cafe.
