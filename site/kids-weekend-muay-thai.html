Grading

How kids move up

  1. Train every weekCome to the Kids Grading Class every Saturday and Sunday. Every hour is tracked in our app, and only grading class hours count.
  2. Reach the hours and the timeEach level needs a set number of grading hours and a minimum time, so skills are solid before moving on.
  3. Pass the gradingEarn 3 stripes, get invited by your coach, and show your skills on grading day. Every new level earns a new prajied and mongkol, presented by our kru.

The six levels

From White Rookie to Phuket King

Hours are counted in Kids Grading Classes since the last level, and each level includes everything from the levels before it. Typical times are for kids coming every Saturday and Sunday.

01

White: Rookie

8 grading hours, at least 1 month (typically month 1). Stance and guard, footwork, jab, cross, teep. 10 push-ups and 30 skips. Bow in and out, count 1 to 10 in Thai, listen to the coach the first time.

02

Yellow: Fighter

24 grading hours, at least 3 months (typically month 4). Hook, uppercut, round kick to the body, straight knee, jab cross kick. 15 push-ups, 1 minute skipping. The Thai names of the 5 weapons. Arrives on time and looks after their own gear.

03

Green: Warrior

36 grading hours, at least 4 months (typically month 9). Horizontal elbow, switch kick, checking a kick, parrying the jab, leaning back. 20 push-ups, 2 minutes skipping, 3 pad rounds. First part of the Wai Kru. Helps set up and pack away.

04

Blue: Striker

48 grading hours, at least 6 months (typically month 15). 4 strike combinations, catch and counter, clinch and knees, light technical sparring for ages 8 and up. 25 push-ups, 4 pad rounds. Basic Muay Thai history and gym rules. Helps new kids.

05

Red: Champion

60 grading hours, at least 6 months (typically month 22). Combinations off defence, low kick, teep variations, clinch turns for ages 13 and up, controlled sparring for ages 8 and up. 30 push-ups, 5 pad rounds. Short Wai Kru Ram Muay. Leads the group warm up.

06

Black and gold: Phuket King

72 grading hours, at least 9 months (typically month 31). Full technical exam of all levels and 5 rounds with the head coach. Full Wai Kru Ram Muay performed in front of parents. Assists in the kids class once a week for a month.

Stripes and age groups

Graded fairly for every age

Stripes: from Yellow up, each level earns 3 stripes in class. Stripe 1 at one third of the hours, stripe 2 at two thirds, and stripe 3 means your child is ready to be invited to grading.

Little Tigers, 5 to 7: graded on technique shape and effort, fitness targets halved, no sparring.

Juniors, 8 to 12: full standard, light technical sparring from Blue.

Teens, 13 to 17: full standard, controlled sparring from Blue, clinch from Red.

Grading days are held every 3 months and are run by Coach Tom and our Thai kru, in English and Thai. Competition is never required.

Questions from parents

What is the difference between this and the regular kids Muay Thai class?

The regular kids Muay Thai classes are just for fun: fitness, skills and enjoying Muay Thai, with no grading. The Kids Grading Class is the official grading training: every hour your child spends in this class counts towards their next level, logged in our app when they check in. See all kids classes.

What age can kids join?

From 5 years old. Our grading has three age groups: Little Tigers (5 to 7), Juniors (8 to 12) and Teens (13 to 17). Message us with your child's age and we will tell you which class suits them best.

Do kids spar?

Not at the start, and never for Little Tigers (5 to 7). Light technical sparring starts at Blue for ages 8 and up, always controlled and with a coach watching closely. Competition is never required.

Do I need to book?

Join the Grading Membership online or at the desk, then come every weekend. Want to try first? A single grading class is 300 THB.

How do I see my child's grading hours?

In the Phuket King app. Sign in with your own email, tap Add a child under My children, and open your child's app to see their level, their grading hours and how many hours are left to the next level. The front desk can link your child to you too. A second parent joins with a code from your app.

What should my child bring?

Comfortable sports clothes and a bottle of water. Message us if your child needs gloves.
