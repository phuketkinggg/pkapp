Why we made it

Arrive ready. Learn faster. Train safer.

Most beginners spend their first week in Thailand just learning how to stand, guard and throw a basic strike. That is time and money spent on things you can learn at home.

Muay Thai Basics gives you those foundations before you step into the gym. You learn the eight weapons of Muay Thai, how to stand and move, how to protect yourself, and the Thai names your coaches use. More importantly, you learn why each technique works, so your first classes go into getting better, not catching up.

It is also the safest way to start. Coach Tom has spent thirty years on injury prevention and movement, and every lesson is built to protect your joints while you learn: correct alignment, balance and control first, power later.

The lessons

Six lessons, the eight limbs

Lesson 1

Stance and guard
How to stand, move and protect yourself. The base for everything else.

Lesson 2

Mad, striking with the hands
Jab, cross, hooks and uppercuts, and how to throw them without dropping your guard.

Lesson 3

Tae, the roundhouse kick
The famous Muay Thai kick: turning the hip, the shin, balance and recovery.

Lesson 4

Sok, the elbow
Short range strikes that make Muay Thai different from every other striking art.

Lesson 5

Khao, the knee
Straight and curved knees, and how they work from distance and in the clinch.

Lesson 6

Basic Muay Thai defence
Blocking, checking kicks and staying safe, so you can train with confidence.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need any experience?

No. The course is made for complete beginners. If you have trained before, it is a great way to clean up your basics.

What do I need at home?

A little space to move and your phone. Gloves or a bag are a bonus, not a must.

How long do I have the lessons?

They are yours. Open them again any time in the app, before your trip or after your classes.

Can I get all the lessons at once?

Yes. We recommend one every other day so each lesson sinks in, but you can choose all lessons at once in the course.

What comes after the course?

Muay Thai Basics is the first 2 weeks of the Phuket King System, our full programme. Come and train with us in Kathu, Phuket, and carry on where the course ends.
