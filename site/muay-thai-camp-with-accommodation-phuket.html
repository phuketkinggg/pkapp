Life at the camp

Questions about staying with us

Is training included with the room?

Yes. When you book your room directly with us, group Muay Thai is included for one person for your whole stay.

Can beginners come to a camp?

Yes. Many guests have never trained before. Coaches teach every level in the same class.

How long do people stay?

From a few days to several months. Many guests come for one or two weeks and extend. Long stays are possible on the DTV visa.

How do I book?

Pick your dates and your bungalow on the stay page and pay securely by card, or message us on WhatsApp.
