How long

Choosing your camp length

There is no wrong length. Most guests come for one or two weeks and many extend once they arrive.

01

1 week

Enough to learn the basics properly: stance, guard, punches, kicks and the teep. Train once or twice a day and recover in between.

02

2 weeks

The most popular stay. Your body adapts, the combinations start to flow and the coaches can really work on your details.

03

1 month

Real progress. Add privates, try light technical sparring if you want, and finish fitter than you have been in years.

04

3 months or more

For people who want to live the life. Long stays are possible on the 5 year DTV visa, and we help with the paperwork.

Packing

What to bring to a Muay Thai camp

  • Shorts and a few t-shirts that dry fast (you will train in heat)
  • Hand wraps and gloves if you have them. Gloves, shin guards and Phuket King shorts are sold at the gym
  • Flip flops, a swimsuit for the lake side and a light rain jacket
  • Any medication you need and a small first aid kit

Life at the camp

Questions about staying with us

Is training included with the room?

Yes. When you book your room directly with us, group Muay Thai is included for one person for your whole stay.

Can beginners come to a camp?

Yes. Many guests have never trained before. Coaches teach every level in the same class.

How long do people stay?

From a few days to several months. Many guests come for one or two weeks and extend. Long stays are possible on the DTV visa.

How do I book?

Pick your dates and your bungalow on the stay page and pay securely by card, or message us on WhatsApp.

How much does a Muay Thai camp in Phuket cost?

At Phuket King you pay for your bungalow and group Muay Thai is included when you book directly. Add recovery from 3,900 THB a week, privates from 900 THB and meals for 650 THB a day. See prices and rooms.

When is the best time for a Muay Thai camp in Phuket?

Any time. November to April is drier and busier. April to October is greener, quieter and our rooms cost less, with short tropical showers that rarely stop training.

Do I need a visa for a long Muay Thai camp?

For stays up to your visa free or tourist days, no. For months of training, the 5 year DTV visa is made for long stays, and Muay Thai is an accepted reason. See our DTV page.
