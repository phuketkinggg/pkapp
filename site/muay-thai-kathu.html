Why Kathu

Quiet enough to train, close to everything

Kathu sits between Patong and Phuket Town, away from the beach crowds but a short drive from the sea, local markets and great Thai food.

At Phuket King you train by the lake, recover in our ice bath and herbal sauna, eat at Pangea Cafe and sleep in a bungalow one minute from the ring. No taxis, no commute.

Questions about training in Kathu

Do I need experience?

No. Every group class is open to all levels. Coaches start beginners with stance, guard and the jab, and build from there at your pace.

How much does a class cost?

A single group class is 400 THB. Weekly, monthly and longer passes cost less per class. See every price on our prices page.

Where exactly are you?

In Kathu, Phuket, by the lake. Open us in Google Maps for directions.

Can I stay at the gym?

Yes. Pangea Resort has eleven air conditioned lakeside bungalows one minute from the ring, and group Muay Thai is included when you book your room with us. See free rooms.
