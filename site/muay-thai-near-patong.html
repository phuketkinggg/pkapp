Getting here

10 to 15 minutes from Patong Beach

Kathu is the next district inland from Patong. Take the road over the hill towards Phuket Town and you are at the gym in 10 to 15 minutes by scooter, taxi or Grab, depending on traffic.

Many people stay in Patong for the beach and nightlife and ride over for the morning or evening class. Others try a week of training, love it, and move to a bungalow at Pangea Resort, one minute from the ring.

Questions from people staying in Patong

How far is Phuket King from Patong?

10 to 15 minutes by road, over the hill into Kathu. A Grab or taxi is easy to get both ways, and many members ride a scooter.

Is it worth leaving Patong to train?

If you want to actually learn Muay Thai, yes. You get a structured class with coaches who teach technique properly, a quieter place to focus, and recovery and food on site.

I have never trained. Can I come?

Yes. Every group class is open to all levels, and coaches start beginners with the basics. Read our beginners guide before your first class.

How much is a class?

A single group class is 400 THB, and weekly, monthly and longer passes cost less per class. See every price on our prices page.

Can I stay at the gym instead?

Yes. Pangea Resort has lakeside bungalows one minute from the ring, and group Muay Thai is included when you book your room with us. See free rooms.
