The training

Technique first, every class

Phuket King is a Muay Thai gym and camp in Kathu, between Patong and Phuket Town. Every weekday has one technical focus, and the morning, afternoon and evening classes all work on it, so you repeat it until it sticks.

  • Monday: kicking
  • Tuesday: punching
  • Wednesday: elbows
  • Thursday: knees and teeps
  • Friday: defence
  • Saturday: sparring technique

Each class has a warm up, technique with a coach, pad rounds, bag work and conditioning. Clinch and fighter technique runs Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at 17:45, and fighters' fitness and running Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 15:30.

Questions about Muay Thai training in Phuket

Do I need to book a group class?

No. Turn up ten minutes before the class and check in at the desk. Privates are booked in advance.

I have never trained. Can I join?

Yes. Every group class is open to all levels and coaches start beginners with stance, guard and the jab. Read Muay Thai for beginners in Phuket.

How much is Muay Thai training in Phuket?

At Phuket King a single class is 400 THB, a week is 2,500 THB and a month is 7,000 THB. Read our guide: how much does Muay Thai cost in Phuket.

Do I have to spar or fight?

No. Sparring is always optional and most guests never fight. Read no, you do not have to fight.

Is there training for kids and women?

Yes. See kids Muay Thai in Phuket and Muay Thai for women in Phuket.

Where are you?

In Kathu, Phuket, by the lake, a short drive from Patong and Phuket Town. Open us in Google Maps.
