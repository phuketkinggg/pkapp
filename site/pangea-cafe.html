Our ethos

Eat to train, eat to recover

Training hard twice a day only works if you eat well. Pangea Cafe is part of the Pangea idea: training, recovery, sleep and food working together, not separately.

  • No processed seed oils. Nothing we cook uses them.
  • Real food. Eggs, meat, fish, fruit, oats and vegetables, made fresh.
  • Built for training. High protein choices, shakes after class and electrolytes for the heat.
  • Simple and honest. Good portions at fair prices.
