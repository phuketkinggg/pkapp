Master the Elbow Entry with Max

You can’t just throw elbows from far away.

You need to create the opening.

In this session, Max shows how to enter for the elbow properly, using a fake teep to force a reaction, close the distance, and hop in with a sharp elbow.

Uses,

🥊 Creating openings
🥊 Closing distance safely
🥊 Using feints effectively
🥊 Timing the elbow entry
🥊 Fight-smart technique

This is a beginner friendly technique, if working with a partner, go slow, protect your partner.
